Scope: the single-page interactive map at web/index.html. Visitor mode: Experience (the map leads from the first viewport; the visitor is inside the work).

Audience and job: North India residents during smog season asking where today's air came from; journalists/researchers secondary; hackathon judges via the live URL and the tour-driven video. Action: scrub a day, click a place or search their town, flip to forward mode. Proof: real season data only (NOAA VIIRS fires, OpenAQ measured PM2.5, Overture districts/towns, Open-Meteo winds). Constraints: neutral, no blame; model data labelled; India political view; mobile on mid-range Android.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The plain is a phulkari cloth and the wind is the thread: every breath a city takes is sewn back across the fields it crossed. It refuses the near-black dashboard map with glowing dots and a stats sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Madder-maroon handspun khaddar ground with a faint weave; indigo-black rivers and borders; fires as gold-silk satin-stitch diamonds; the traced air as white running stitch with nine faint sibling threads; forward smoke as rose thread; PM2.5 bands as an embroidered border of stitched columns. Khand display caps, Mukta body, tabular figures that tick. Controls are cloth tags edged in running stitch; the active one is satin-filled.

STORY: The visitor meets Delhi on a smog day, watches its air sewn back over Punjab's fires, scrubs the border to a clean day when the thread comes off the hills, searches their own town, flips forward to see a fire's smoke settle over towns, and leaves understanding "air that crossed fires was dirtier", not who to blame.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cloth map of the plain. Top-left plaque: city name in Khand, a large ticking PM2.5 numeral, date and hour. The 48 h thread animates out from Delhi to Punjab while gold diamonds brighten under it. A full-width embroidered timeline border sits along the bottom edge. Search tag top-right; "Play the story" tag beside the plaque is the primary action.

FORM: Phulkari (Punjabi field embroidery, bagh ground), position 5 of the ordered list, seed key 29f1f807.

Raises: motion is physics, threads only follow the real hourly wind (from ink basin); one active edge, only the traced path is bright (from cloud quarry); headline counts tick in fixed figures (from nixie counter); the tour advances along the wind's line (from agit-train); a ruled caption names every layer and its source (from sapeur plate).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
